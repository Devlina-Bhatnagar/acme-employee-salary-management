# Demo script (2–3 minutes)

1. Open the deployed service and log in with your locally configured HR administrator credentials.
2. On Dashboard, point out headcount and **currency-separated** totals; filter Germany + Engineering and view breakdown.
3. Switch to Employees. Search by name/employee code, filter by country/department, and demonstrate server-side pagination.
4. Open an employee; show current annual base salary and dated salary history. Change salary with a reason, save, and show new history item and dashboard update.
5. Add a new employee, then deactivate them (show that salary history remains intact).
6. Export current filtered results; inspect the CSV. Import a few fresh employees with the sample template and confirm duplicates/invalid rows are rejected atomically.
7. Show tests, docs, incremental commits and Docker configuration. Explicitly mention intentional exclusions: no FX conversion, payroll or speculative AI insight.

To record: use OBS Studio or screen capture at 1280×720, keeping test credentials out of the video. A recorded walkthrough is not fabricated by this repository.
